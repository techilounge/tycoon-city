# Obvious Autobuild orientation
Tycoon City is a premium, original, multiplayer property-trading strategy game. This repo intentionally starts as a runnable local UI/demo, not a production multiplayer game.
Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PRODUCT.md`, and `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Implement the roadmap incrementally. Prioritize the server-authoritative engine, room lifecycle, reconnect, responsive UX, and deterministic tests before 3D visuals, payments, or AI features. Use PRs and require human review for architectural/security changes.
